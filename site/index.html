Questions

Before you connect

Does replying to reviews actually help?

In a peer-reviewed study published in Marketing Science, hotels that started replying to their TripAdvisor reviews went on to get 12% more reviews, and their average rating rose by 0.12 stars (INFORMS summary). That study looked at hotels, so results for your business may differ. Customers notice too: in BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US consumers, 80% said they're more likely to use a business that responds to all of its reviews (summary).

Do you write or post fake reviews?

Never. We only reply to reviews real customers left. Writing, buying or hiding reviews breaks FTC rules and Google's policies, and we don't do any of it.

Will replies sound like a robot?

Half of consumers are put off by generic, templated replies, according to the same BrightLocal survey. So each reply refers to what the customer actually said and follows the tone you pick. You can read every reply in your monthly summary and change the tone anytime.

What happens with a bad review?

On the Pro and Premium plans, you pick how these are handled. With approval on, replies to 1 to 3 star reviews come to you by email first, and you can approve, edit, or ask us to skip it. With auto-reply on, we post a calm, apologetic reply right away. Either way, reviews that mention a legal threat, an injury or a refund dispute always come to you first. We never admit fault or share customer details on your behalf.

Do you work with medical and dental practices?

Yes, and their replies follow HIPAA privacy rules. A reply never confirms that the reviewer is a patient, and never mentions a visit, treatment, condition or any other health detail, even if the reviewer brought it up first. Instead, the practice thanks them in general terms and invites them to call the office to talk privately. For practices, replies to 1 to 3 star reviews come to you for approval by default. HHS has fined dental practices for revealing patient details in review replies, so we take this seriously.

Will you reply to my older reviews?

Yes. During your free trial we answer every new review, plus up to 10 of your most recent unanswered ones so you can see the quality. Once you subscribe, we work through the rest of your backlog, newest and lowest-rated first, at a steady pace of 5 to 7 a day so your profile looks natural.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, in one click. There's no contract. Disconnecting also removes our access to your Google profile.

Will replying to reviews make Claude or Google's AI recommend me?

Nobody can promise that, and we won't. What we know is that customers read your replies, Google says replies show you're responsive, and businesses AI tools recommend tend to have strong ratings. Google's AI answers pull from the Google Business Profile, where your reviews and replies live, for nearly every local business (BrightLocal, 2026). On the Premium plan we check each month whether Claude names you, so you can see where you stand.
