Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026. Reply Ally is run by Reply Ally LLC, New York.

What we collect

When you ask for a free review check or join the trial list, we collect your business name, what you do, your neighborhood or town, your email address, and whether you want our weekly tip. We also look at your business's public Google reviews to prepare your review check.

How we use it

  • To send the review check you asked for, and to contact you about your trial.
  • To send the weekly tip, only if you ticked that box. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that run this site and send our email.

Where it's stored

Your details are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this site. We keep them until you ask us to delete them, or for up to 2 years after we last hear from you.

Your choices

Email hello@replyally.net to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, and we'll do it within 30 days.

When you connect your Google profile

Once your trial starts, connecting your Google Business Profile lets us read your reviews and post replies for you. We use that access only for those replies, and disconnecting removes it. We'll update this page with the details before any business connects.
